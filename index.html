Infantry Esports future plan

Roadmap

Infantry Esports is a growing competitive gaming community within British Army Esports. This plan is about raising awareness, improving recruitment and engagement, and giving players a polished, organised home in Discord, for competitive and casual players alike.

3 of 5goals complete
  1. ✓
    Complete

    Finish building the Discord server

    Rules, suggestions and support channels give the server a professional feel, so new players know they're joining an established community, not just a group chat.

  2. ✓
    Complete

    Game Leads and game channels

    Each game has a Game Lead who runs and organises it, so new players know exactly who to approach to get involved.

  3. ✓
    Complete

    Regimental roles

    Members pick their regiment, so we can see where the community is coming from and which regiments and battalions need more awareness.

  4. 4
    To do

    Esports news channel

    Keep players up to date with what's happening across British Army Esports and BFBS Esports, make sure nobody misses an opportunity, and get conversations going.

  5. 5
    To do

    Reaching regulars and reserves

    Competitions are open to regulars and reserves, so we need to reach both. Regimental contacts can help with posters and word of mouth, and every reserve location has a point of contact we can ask to put posters up.
